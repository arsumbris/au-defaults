# Welcome to Ars Umbris

This is the default workspace: your files on the left, documents in the middle, and agent sessions on the right.

## Take a look around

Open [[Workspace guide]] to learn what each area does. Then open [[Getting started]] for a short walkthrough.

The map below Files shows connections in the workspace. Open a note and explore its links; you can keep multiple documents in tabs.

## Make it yours

Settings contains appearance and configuration controls. Your layout and shortcuts are ordinary files: `studio.yaml` and `navigation.keymap.yaml`.

The session area includes Claude Code and Codex profiles. Running an agent requires the adapter and its account setup; creating this workspace does not sign you in or start a session.

## What belongs to you

These starter files were copied into this folder. You can edit or delete them without changing the template.

Extra sources you connect are different: they stay in their original folders. Editable sources share your edits with other workspaces using those folders; Reference sources provide content and tools without becoming editable here.
