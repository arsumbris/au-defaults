# Packages

The Ars Umbris packages this workspace brings together. Each entry links to that package's own guide.

## au-base-types

Entry: [[README::au-base-types]]

Shared types give knowledge records a common vocabulary across packages.
Build your own types for the distinctions your work needs.

## au-ingest

Entry: [[README::au-ingest]]

Convert archived documents and PDFs into readable source notes while keeping the originals.
Add converters for other kinds of material you collect.

## au-tree-research

Entry: [[README::au-tree-research]]

Develop a subject through research questions and saved articles.
The articles can become sources for other work, including a woven knowledge graph.

## au-weave

Entry: [[README::au-weave]]

Turn source material into connected knowledge with links back to evidence.
Use the supplied concepts, claims and entities, or define an extraction profile for your subject.

## au-agent-guides

Entry: [[README::au-agent-guides]]

Create guides that explain your practices and when to apply them.
Your agent can help write them and consult them when similar work comes up.

## au-writing-style

Entry: [[README::au-writing-style]]

Create a writing profile suited to your work.
Reuse or write rules, then apply the profile to prose or check how well it fits.

## au-skills

Entry: [[README::au-skills]]

Turn recurring tasks into skills your agent can invoke.
Authoring and independent review help you check whether the instructions improve the work.

## au-rules

Entry: [[README::au-rules]]

Choose standing habits for how agents work with your graph.
Create your own rules and bundle them into a profile for future sessions.

## au-govern

Entry: [[README::au-govern]]

Review knowledge against the workspace's purpose and supporting evidence.
Semantic checks judge meaning and record findings that repair skills can address.

## au-competency

Entry: [[README::au-competency]]

Keep the workspace's purpose and use recurring feedback to develop agreed changes.
Research missing methods when they could change what you build.

## au-mcp-sdk

Entry: [[README::au-mcp-sdk]]

Build tools and runtime hooks, or supply context agents receive at session start.
Shared contracts let your extensions work with the agent framework.
